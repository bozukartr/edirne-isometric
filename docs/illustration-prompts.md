# Illustration prompts and source assets

Built-in image generation was used to restyle original open-data scene renders. No API/CLI fallback, satellite imagery, or reference-site images were used as generation inputs.

## Saved project assets

All five final source illustrations are in `assets/illustrations/`: `city-overview.webp`, `north.webp`, `south.webp`, `east.webp`, and `historic-center.webp`. Their geographic rectangles and native pixel sizes are recorded in `assets/illustrations/regions.json`. The deployed tile pyramid, overview, and twelve thumbnails are in `dist/atlas-v4/`.

`npm run guides` recreates the original composition guides from the OSM/Microsoft database. `npm run atlas` encodes the atlas from the committed illustrations without generation or network requests. PNG-to-WebP conversion, overlap feathering, and tile/thumbnail packaging are publication processing. All source generation images are retained separately from the packaged tiles.

## Historic center: initial style illustration

Input role: the original `historic-center` open-data render, 3840 × 2160. Final output: `assets/illustrations/historic-center.webp`.

```text
Use case: style-transfer. Asset: a high-resolution, geographically registered illustrated map tile for an interactive isometric map of Edirne, Turkey. Edit the supplied original open-data city render into an extraordinarily detailed, richly textured, believable hand-painted aerial miniature city illustration, like a premium illustrated isometric city atlas. THIS IS A MAP RESTYLING: preserve the exact image bounds, camera angle, projection, road centerlines, every river bank and bridge position, the location and scale of all building footprints, and all landmark positions. Do not recenter, crop, rotate, zoom, add waterways, move landmarks, invent streets, or change the geographic layout. Cover the entire rectangular canvas edge to edge. Replace the simplistic flat polygon appearance with naturally shaded red terracotta roof tiles, varied weathered plaster and stone facades, tiny windows and balconies, soft ambient occlusion between buildings, fine cast shadows, dense textured rounded tree canopies, realistic green gardens, subdued asphalt and pale stone paving, and finely rippled teal river water. Keep all existing empty fields and roads in their exact locations. The distinctive mosque just above and right of the middle is Edirne Selimiye: faithfully recognizable large lead-grey central dome and FOUR slender minarets, with a courtyard, without enlarging its footprint. The smaller mosque to its southwest is Eski Cami with nine domes; preserve its position. Other mosques remain in place. Aim for tangible architectural depth and photographic material detail while retaining an elegant illustrated miniature quality, rather than a satellite photograph or a blocky videogame render. Neutral natural late-morning daylight from upper left, consistent across the whole canvas; subdued natural greens, warm masonry, muted terracotta and zinc-grey domes. Very sharp fine detail, no blur, no depth of field, no outlines, no pixelation, no paper texture. NO text, labels, UI, markers, borders, compass, vignette or watermark. High-resolution 3840 by 2160 landscape output, identical 16:9 framing to the input.
```

The generation returned a 1672 × 941 illustration. Requested resolution is not a claim about the resulting file size. Native dimensions are recorded for every source image. The rest of the map uses this original Edirne illustration as a style reference only.

## Accuracy

Prompts request geographic preservation, but generative details and registration remain approximate. Review is visual; this is not surveyed or navigational imagery. Footprint counts refer to the input database. Architectural heights, facades, textures, vegetation, and some fine geometry are artistic interpretations. All twelve curated landmarks are covered by a detailed source region, and the runtime uses the same fixed projection as the composition guides.
