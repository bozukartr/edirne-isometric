Hello, Edirne.

A little atlas for getting wonderfully lost. Explore the historic center, cross the Meriç and Tunca, and follow the city all the way to Karaağaç.

The map behind the rooftops

Landmark models are artistic interpretations. Building heights are estimated where height data is unavailable. This is an exploration map, rather than a navigation service.
